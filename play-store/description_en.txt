LoopMuse remembers the songs you like and have played, and brings music to your alarms.

Mark songs you like, revisit tracks you have played, and keep your listening order. Play music stored on your device and use it with daily alarms.

• Keep the current track, playback order, and played-song history for each playlist.
• Choose sequential, smart, or random playback, with selected songs, liked songs, and single-track repeat.
• Review played songs and find a track you heard earlier.
• Use your music with alarms.
• Discover music online, search for lyrics, and share short song recommendations in the Lounge.

Playback of files on your device and alarms are local features. Discovery, lyrics search, and the Lounge need an internet connection. A Google sign-in is required when you first use the app.
